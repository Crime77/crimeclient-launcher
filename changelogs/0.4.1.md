## Launcher Changelog v0.4.1

### Fixes
- **CrimeClient Insider Profile** - The profile no longer stays hidden when Mojang briefly refuses the sign-in (too many requests). The launcher asks again by itself after a minute and a few more times after that, and while Mojang refuses, the public Insider list decides
- **Fewer Sign-ins** - The CrimeClient sign-in (shop and Insider) is kept for 30 days, so starting the launcher no longer signs in to Mojang every time, and two checks at once share one sign-in

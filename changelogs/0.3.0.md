## Launcher Changelog v0.3.0

### New Features
- **New Design** - The whole launcher has a new, calm look: flat dark surfaces, thin lines, a wide display font and numbers in mono. The accent colour now only marks PLAY, the page you are on and progress
- **Sidebar** - Play, Profiles, Mods, Skins, Shop and Clips sit in a fixed sidebar on the left, with your account, the settings and the version at the bottom
- **Start Page** - The home server in one line with players and ping, your player in the middle, the news as a list on the right and a launch bar with the profile and a PLAY button that fills with the progress
- **Shop With Real Pictures** - Every cosmetic in the shop shows a rendered picture of itself instead of an icon
- **Linux** - The launcher now runs on Linux too, as an AppImage that updates itself and as a deb package. CrimeClient Rust profiles pick the package for your system

### Improvements
- **Profiles As A Table** - Profiles, mods, worlds, servers and logs are dense rows with their details in mono; actions show when you point at a row
- **Settings** - Two columns: sections on the left, rows on the right
- **Fewer Effects** - The Matrix, fog, retro grid and ember backgrounds are gone; Calm, a faint grid or your own picture or video remain
- **Smaller Windows** - Every page works at 1000 x 640, the version lists and toolbars keep their columns readable

### Fixes
- **Modpack Changelogs** - The version list of a modpack no longer shows made-up changelogs for versions that have none
- **Language** - The account restriction notice follows the language you picked, also after you change it
